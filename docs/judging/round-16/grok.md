UI SCORE
U1 Visual hierarchy & layout: 5/5: At 1440×900 the header sits at y=12 (h=90), the KPI bar at y=114 (h=55) and the toolbar at y=181 (h=56). Toggling prospect-safe does not move any of them. The plan is the next surface: one legend, then the stack or the building. With no floor open, each label’s centre matches its floor anchor within 0.2 px (Floor 12 both at 478; Floor 1 both at 758.8). Floor 8’s heading is only “21,400 RSF leasable”; leased and available sit in the cards under the plate. Opening a floor docks the model beside a 76 px picker and does not cover the toolbar.
U2 Color & data encoding: 4.5/5: Status uses fill plus hatch, dots, dashes and words (sublease, committed, lobby, holdover, vacant). The Building legend includes “Lobby · building use 8,000”. Neighbouring expiration steps are at least 8.1 L* apart and differ in hue in Ascendix night, Ascendix day, Vice night and Vice day (the tight pair is Ascendix day exp-4 `#d6e1fa` to exp-5 `#f3f9fc`, 8.1 L* and 39°). The half point: that same daytime exp-5 fill is L* 97.6 against a white card (`#ffffffb8`), so on the light expiration stack floors 9–11 read as barely tinted blocks. The edge `#5a91b3` and the dark text `#292d3b` (`src/styles/ascendix.css:162`) keep them from vanishing, which is why this is not a full point. Night and Vice steps are 9.8 L* or more apart.
U3 Typography & density: 4/4: Floor labels are 13 px in the Building list and the Map list, and the two lists print the same line, including “7 · 100.0% leased · 7,000 SF sublease available” and “Largest block”. A scan for clipped text at 1440 found only the screen-reader nodes. Long names wrap (North American Asset Holdings) instead of ellipsing. Phone facts break on the separator; the leading “·” is in the text node and clipped by `.sp-dotrun`, so a line does not end on it. Rates keep their unit and type (`$34–$36/SF/yr NNN`, `$3.25/SF/mo NNN`).
U4 Polish & platform fit: 3/3: Glass skin in night, day and Vice City, with a theme switch that does not restyle print. Scrubbing the dock, the floor pane stays at opacity 0 while the model’s box still crosses it, and by 200 ms the model (right edge 514) is clear of the pane (left edge 562). The picker never intersects the model. After Whole building the floor list is opacity 1 at 250 ms with no overlap. Hover, selected, dimmed (35%) and the Largest block tag are consistent. Map console stayed empty after the tiles loaded.
U5 Mobile & print visual quality: 3/3: The 390 px stack is full-width rows with the name, suite, SF and rate readable. The owner PDFs are 3 pages and the prospect-safe PDF is 2; the safe cover is “Section 1 of 2”, the footnote is on that page, tenant names are “Tenant”, and asking rents remain. The owner expiry cover is “Section 1 of 3” and fills the sheet. The 7 px phone and 56 px tablet overlap of a selected suite is scored under X2, where the scroll behaviour fails.

UX SCORE
X1 Persona job coverage: 5/5: Occupancy 86.1% with 80.2% occupied and 8,000 SF signed-not-in; available 33,200 (26,200 direct · 7,000 sublease); largest block 9,300 on Floor 12, tagged on the plan. Expiration lens, rollover buckets and the 12-month worklist answer the renewal sweep. As-of 2028 is in the URL and recomputes the plan. Find 12,000 SF returns one option (Floor 6, Ste 600, from Apr 2027) and names near misses with their shortfall. Issues, the unplaced lease and the “+500 SF over” on Floor 7 are on the plan. Deals show stage and prospect (LOI · Vantage Robotics, Proposal · Northwind Health). Prospect-safe is a header switch and a two-page PDF.
X2 Interaction & information architecture: 3.5/4: One floor list is the picker. ArrowDown moves Floor 12 to Floor 11, Enter opens that floor and focus stays on the picker row (“11 100.0%”), Escape returns to the label. Whole building restores the list in a quarter of a second. Finder copy names only the rows it shows (“smaller spaces with their shortfall, and stacked floors that combine direct and sublease space” beside the 12,000 SF search, which lists Floors 7–8 and does not re-list Floor 6). Prospect-safe drops as-of, lens, worklist, search and inquiry from the URL (`?view=stack&safe=1`) and puts them back (`asOf=2028-06-01&window=12&inquiry=I02&q=vantage`). The miss: growing the bottom sheet does not keep the selection clear. At half height the gap is the intended 16 px (phone sheet top 439, suite bottom 424; tablet sheet top 399, block bottom 383). At full height, after the 180 ms height transition, the phone suite ends at 75 under a sheet that starts at 68, and on a 1024 px tablet the selected Ste 350 block runs from y=8 to y=117 under a sheet that starts at y=61, so the lower half of the selected suite is covered. `scrollY` still had room (phone 2283 of 2954, tablet 1134 of 1432). `useClearOfSheet` scrolls inside the transition (`src/components/stack/aboveSheet.ts:48-55`) and nothing runs again when the height transition ends (`src/components/ui/ui.css:144-146`).

Prospect-safe across Building, Stack, Timeline, Map, Floor 1, a selected block and Find contained none of the tenant, contact or broker names; the building sale is “Listed for sale” with no $68,500,000; the condo asking price stays. X4 Accessibility of interaction: 3/3: The floor list is one roving tab stop; focus never fell to the body while a floor was opened from the keyboard or closed with Whole building. Daytime expiration text is `#292d3b` or `#1f0e00` on the pale steps and white on `#b03a16`.
X5 CRM workflows & differentiation: 4/4: The deals lens, inquiry match (Northwind Health, Kestrel Labs), last-touch badges (Call 40 d, Tour 25 d, Meeting 7 d, No activity), the 30-day change list, data issues and record actions (Open availability, New deal, Log a call) are the CRM objects VTS and CoStar do not sit on. Prospect-safe is the whole screen and the PDF, not a second hidden view. Record actions are toast stubs, which the brief allows. Multi-building is out of scope for this prototype.
UX TOTAL: 19.5/20

ENGINEERING (informational): 8.5/10: Domain code is pure TypeScript with the prospect-safe rules, finder exclusions and floor-label text in one place, and the live numbers match that model. Components are split by view, styles are token CSS, and the URL, title and root flags go through `src/lib/host.ts` (`usePlanState.ts`). The half point off is the unfinished boundary: `src/state/focusStore.ts` and `src/App.tsx` still call `document` and `window` directly for focus, print and keyboard, so a Lightning host cannot replace those without editing the shell.
