UI SCORE
U1 Visual hierarchy & layout: 4.5/5: The order is clear: header, six KPI cards, one toolbar, then the plan card with the legend in its head. The Building view's two columns (model and picker on the left; floor head, plate, numbers and tabs on the right) read well at 1440×1100 (shot 33). On real laptop heights the landing screen does not fit. I measured it live: at 1440×900 the model svg starts at y=432 and is 600 px tall, so floors 1–4 and the floor KPIs are below the fold. At 1280×800 the svg is 500 px tall from y=451, and the picker is cut off after Floor 3. The cause is building.css:15, `height: clamp(420px, calc(100vh - 300px), 640px)`, which assumes about 300 px of chrome when there is about 430. With the inspector open (select=A02 at 1280) the "Unpin" and "Full floor plan" buttons wrap onto a second line under the heading, with "Pinned" floating below them. Below about 1000 px the Building tab disappears and the app lands on Stack, which is the right fallback.
U2 Color & data encoding: 4.5/5: The neon-on-purple status lens is restrained. Teal means leaseable, amber means leaving, red means at risk. The new leased fill (rgba(78,68,134,.9) with a 0.42 lavender edge) now reads as blocks against the card (shot 00). Sublease dots, committed hatch, dashed vacant and the Holdover/MTM/Confidential/Signed badges all carry meaning without colour. The round-10 fix landed: the model now has dots and hatch overlays. The legend gives SF and share for each state. UX SCORE
X1 Persona job coverage: 5/5: All 10 jobs can be done fast. Vacancy and occupancy come from the KPIs and lens. Rollover from the chart and buckets. Issues are surfaced (8, with locate). X2 Interaction & IA: 3.5/4: I drove it live. Pointing previews a floor ("Preview · click the floor to pin it"). A click pins it (?floor=F11), lifts the floors above and survives the pointer travelling to the plate. Hovering another floor previews it and leaving returns to the pinned floor. A plate click selects the suite and opens the inspector. A second click unpins, and there is an explicit Unpin. The deductions: landing on Floor 12 "Most space available" is sensible, but the landing screen hides half its own content at 1440×900 (see U1). Floor focus in the Stack says "Nothing available" on Floor 6 even though the inspector shows it marketed from Apr 2027 (shot 04).

X4 Accessibility of interaction: 2.5/3: Strong in places. The skip link works and the focus order is logical. Plate suite names end in a bare deal count ("…$38–$40/SF/yr FSG 1").
X5 CRM workflows & differentiation: 4/4: Things VTS, CoStar and Yardi cannot show on a plan: live deals by stage on each vacancy and as renewals, last-touch badges and the 12-month no-activity flag, inquiries that fit with click-to-highlight, record actions (Open lease, New deal, Log call, Start renewal), a 30-day change history on the block, stale-record and stale-deal issues, and marketed-for-later supply. The Building view's tabs put deals, inquiries and issues for each floor one click from the model.

ENGINEERING (informational): 8/10: The domain is pure TypeScript with roughly 300 vitest cases, including a new segmentText.test.ts that pins the 12/24-month badge rule and the safe suppression. Prospect-safe masking goes through one module (safe.ts), and that is exactly why the FloorLists leak stands out: the Deals and Issues tabs skip those helpers, and none of the leak scripts click tabs. The BuildingHero viewBox fit (it tracks drawn extents and pads for the lift and tags) is a tidy fix for the round-10 dead space. The URL-synced state makes every state linkable.
