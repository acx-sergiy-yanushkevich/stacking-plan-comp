U2 Color & data encoding: 4.5/5: Night status is not colour-only: teal available, amber expires, rose holdover, dots on sublease, hatch on committed, dashed vacant, and badges (Holdover, MTM, Confidential, Signed not in, Avail Apr '27). The legend carries SF and share. Map floors are flat-lit and the floor list names the state in words ("Half available", "Expiring", "Leased"), so the extrusion is not the only cue. Deduction: by day the expiration ramp does not step in hue. The light Vice block never overrides the fills, so the page keeps the token ramp commented "one hue, lightness ordered" (`src/styles/tokens.css` 48–54). Computed day fills are #9c3b18, #b9531f, #e98c3c, #f5b86c, #fbdca6, #fff4db at hues 16°, 20°, 28°, 33°, 38°, 42°. The 5+ yr cream sits on a cream card and is separated by its edge, not by a different hue (live `?theme=light&lens=expiry`).
U3 Typography & density: 4/4: Figures are tabular and exact (86.1%, 33,200 SF, 9,300 SF, 35,300 SF, WALT 2.98 yr). A live walk of stack text found no ellipsis. Long names wrap or drop to the next tier instead of being cut: phone cards show "Kinetic Ventures" and "Anadarko Drilling" in full with suite, SF and expiry (`src/components/stack/stack.css` 93 `overflow-wrap: anywhere`). The WALT card shows two decimals and the tooltip the exact figure: "2.9776 years (35.73 months), to four decimals".
U4 Polish & platform fit: 3/3: The glass skin is one language in night and day, with its own theme switch, and print stays plain white. Hover, selected, dimmed and empty states are drawn. Opening a floor runs a FLIP on `.sp-bview__stage` (Web Animations, duration 760, `BuildingView.tsx` 85) while the hero angle tweens 760 ms (`BuildingHero.tsx` 31) and the floor column slides in 520 ms after a 140 ms delay (`building.css` 26–27). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` those animations are absent, the path jumps once, and it does not keep moving. Lifted floors on the stage are outlines (`hero.css` 44–51; live, 6 outline tops with Floor 8 open). A find filter turns non-matches neutral. Translucency did not swallow the type I read on the plan, the KPIs or the phone cards.
U5 Mobile & print visual quality: 3/3: At 390 px the KPI strip is a 3-column grid with every figure on screen and no label overflow; at 768 and 1024 it is one row of six. The phone timeline stacks a full label above each bar (shot 25 and live). The phone stack is a card per floor, readable without horizontal scroll. The Letter landscape PDFs are a real report (cover with KPIs and the plan, rent roll, rollover and worklist), not a screenshot of the app, and the on-screen print emulation matches that structure (shot 28).

UX TOTAL: 19.5/20

ENGINEERING (informational): 9/10: `npm test` is 335 tests in 33 files, all passing, covering KPIs, the finder, lenses, issues, the safe projection and the building view. Domain logic is pure TypeScript with no React imports; `src/domain/safe.ts` is the only prospect-safe policy and the UI asks it instead of re-deciding. State is one URL-synced hook. Components are split by view with one CSS file each and tokens rather than CSS-in-JS, which is the right shape for an LWC port. The points held back are the MapLibre extrusion, the canvas text measurer and the rAF building tween: they are correct in the browser and awkward in a Salesforce shadow tree, and they are isolated enough that the rest of the app can move without them.

TOP UX FIXES (ranked by points gained per effort, max 5):
