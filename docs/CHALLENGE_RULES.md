# Vice City Mission: The Real Estate Rush. Official rules

Ascendix Tech Challenge, November 2026. Transcribed from `Vice_City_Mission_Official_Rules_Updated.pdf` (3 pages, updated Nov 12, 2026).

> Welcome to Vice City! It's time to take on a real business challenge, experiment with new tools, and show us what you can build.

## 1. The mission

- **Challenge:** help brokers understand building availability and leasing options more easily.
- **Task:** build a prototype that makes available spaces and leasing opportunities easier to explore.
- It must be a **prototype**, not a fully developed or production-ready application.
- **Ideas:** interactive stacking plans, 3D visualizations, AI-assisted tools, leasing scenario simulators, or your own idea.
- Time spent on the challenge is **not tracked as working hours**.

## 2. Who can join

- Everyone at Ascendix, solo or as a team.
- No technical background or ready-made idea required.
- Choose your own tools and technologies.

## 3. Data sources

- Publicly available datasets.
- Mock data you create yourself.
- Authorized company data.
- **State your assumptions.**
- No dataset is provided. Researching the building is part of the challenge.
- **Don't use the current AscendixRE stacking plan as a reference.** The organizers want a fresh take.
- **Never** use confidential company, customer or personal information without permission.

## 4. What to submit

| Deliverable | Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Demo video** | MP4, max **1 minute**, 1080p or lower. Shows your prototype. **AI-modified voiceover**. |
| **README** | PDF preferred, **1–2 pages**. The problem. Your solution. Tools you used. Your assumptions. **What works and what is mocked.** |
| **Development plan** | PDF or slides, about 1 page or a few slides. **Where you'd take the prototype next.** How it could become a real feature or product. |
| **Prototype** | A single interactive HTML file, or a hosted link. Must open **without installing anything or adding API keys**. |

## 5. How to submit (due **November 18, end of day**)

1. Create a **separate folder** on SharePoint.
2. Upload **all four deliverables**: demo video, README, development plan and prototype (file or link).
3. Check that all files and links **work** and **can be opened**, including the prototype, without installs or API keys.
4. Copy the **folder link** and submit it through the **form** provided by Iryna Tkachuk.

## 6. Most important rule: anonymous submissions

You are responsible for making sure your submitted materials don't reveal your identity.

- Use an **AI-modified voice** instead of your real voice in the demo.
- **Don't mention your name** or your team members' names.
- Remove **usernames, profile pictures and names** from the video, README and prototype, **including the prototype link and page footer**.

You don't need to anonymize folder names or submission form entries. The organizers will anonymize everything before sharing projects with judges and voters.

## 7. Tools and reimbursement

- The company reimburses **up to $125 per participant** for paid tools used to create the prototype.
- Follow the Invoice Reimbursement Procedure.
- Email subject: `Tech Challenge – Tool Invoice – Tool Name – Name Surname – Month YYYY`

## Compliance checklist for this project

- [ ] Demo video is MP4, ≤ 60 s, 1080p or lower, shows the prototype, AI-modified voiceover.
- [ ] README is a 1–2 page PDF covering problem, solution, tools, assumptions, and what works and what is mocked.
- [ ] Separate development plan (about 1 page): next steps and the path to a real feature or product.
- [ ] Prototype is a hosted link or a single HTML file that opens with no install and no API keys.
- [ ] No names, usernames or profile pictures in the video, README or prototype. The hosted URL (domain and project name) and the page footer count too. Check git-derived text, screenshots, `docs/`, and any author or byline strings shipped in the build.
- [ ] Only mock or public data; assumptions stated.
- [ ] All four deliverables uploaded to their own SharePoint folder; links open; form submitted by Nov 18 end of day.
- [ ] UX is designed for both brokers **and** buyers / potential tenants (the Best UX criterion).
